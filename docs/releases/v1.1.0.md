# ACRUX 1.1: Auto Mix

**Turn on Auto Mix and ACRUX mixes one song into the next, the way a DJ would.** It listens to each song once, finds
its tempo, beats, bars, key and where its intro and outro are, then picks the right way into the next song and plays
it to the sample.

- 🎚️ **Beat-matched blends.** When two songs are close in tempo, the next one comes in on the phrase, locked to the
  beat. The basses swap on a downbeat, and the song going out speeds up or slows down a little in its last bars, like
  a record under a DJ's finger.
- 🎛️ **The right move for every pair.** Songs too far apart in tempo get an echo out or a filter sweep. Music without
  a beat gets a natural fade, and a song that stops dead gets a tight segue. Clashing keys get a short overlap.
- 🗣️ **No two voices at once.** A blend never lays one singer over another. It gets shorter or moves instead.
- 💿 **Albums stay whole.** Play an album in order and its songs follow each other with no gap, as the artist meant.
- 🔊 **Level all the way through.** Every song is brought to the same loudness while Auto Mix is on.
- ♾️ **Infinite flows better.** The songs Infinite adds are put in the order that mixes best: tempo, key and energy.

Turn it on with the **Auto Mix** button at the top of the queue. Its two crossing curves move like a crossfader: under
your pointer, when you turn it on, and during every mix, as it happens.

## Already have ACRUX 1.0?

It updates itself. ACRUX downloads 1.1 in the background and puts a dot on **Settings**. Press **Restart now** there,
or just quit and open it again. With Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask acrux`.
