# ACRUX 1.2: Your Downloaded Songs, and Song Features

**Everything you can play without the internet, on one page. Auto Mix that fits every genre. And ACRUX now learns
every song you play, getting ready for a visualizer.**

- ⬇️ **Your Downloaded Songs.** It's in the middle of the Library dial, where Favorites was (Favorites is still in
  Playlists). It lists every song that plays offline:
  - your downloads;
  - your own files and local playlists, each as one card instead of a long list;
  - the Drive songs that are whole in the cache, at the bottom. Each has an ⏳ and the number of days before it leaves
    the cache, in red on its last day.
- 🎛️ **Auto Mix for every genre.** We checked it on In Rainbows and on 17 genres, from disco, techno and hip-hop to
  folk, jazz, metal and a waltz. Now:
  - a song that fades out is mixed inside its own fade;
  - a blend never skips a last verse that's still being sung;
  - a live-played song isn't sped up enough for its pitch to show;
  - a cut waits for the song's last hit.
- 🧠 **Song Features.** ACRUX works out each song once and keeps what it finds:
  - its beats, downbeats and time signature, found with [Beat This!](https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this), a
    beat-tracking model from ISMIR 2024 that runs on your computer;
  - its sections and their repeats, key, loudness, energy and where the voice is;
  - how its sound changes from moment to moment.

  This work happens in the background and never interrupts the music. While nothing is playing, ACRUX goes through
  the songs already on your computer, one at a time. It never downloads a song just to study it, and it stops as
  soon as you press play. A visualizer will be built on this.
- ☁️ **Google Drive is sturdier:**
  - a weak connection no longer ends the song;
  - importing a folder no longer gets stuck at *Reading songs · 0 of 18* while its covers download;
  - removing a folder and adding it back reads it again;
  - setup moves on by itself once you've signed in to Google in the browser.

## Already have ACRUX?

It updates itself. ACRUX downloads 1.2 in the background and puts a dot on **Settings**. Press **Restart now** there,
or just quit and open it again. The download is about 12 MB bigger than before, because the beat-tracking model comes
with it. With Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask acrux`.
