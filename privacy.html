ACRUX privacy

ACRUX is a music player that runs on your own computer. Whoever runs it is the only one who holds its data.

Google Drive

When you sign in to Google Drive, ACRUX asks for read-only access to your Drive (drive.readonly). It uses it only to list the folders you add and to play the songs in them. It never changes, deletes or shares anything in your Drive.

Your sign-in is kept on the computer running ACRUX: in the macOS Keychain, or elsewhere in a private file in its data folder. Songs you play are kept in a cache on that computer.

What leaves your computer

Only requests to the services you use: Google (YouTube and Drive), Jamendo, Audius, the Internet Archive and iTunes, to search and play music. ACRUX has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your listening history stays on your computer.

Stopping access

Sign out in ACRUX (My Music, at the bottom), or remove ACRUX from your Google account’s connected apps.
